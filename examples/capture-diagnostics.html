Capture diagnostics

Exercises Camera/Scanner's native capture path — the one a real camera actually uses — and checks it stays alive under sustained sampling.

Why this page exists. app.html's self-test cannot cover this. It fakes a camera with canvas.captureStream(), which produces BGRA frames; Camera only takes its native luma path for NV12/I420, which is what real camera hardware produces. So the self-test silently exercises the canvas/RGBA fallback and passes even when the native path is completely broken. A capture bug that killed real-camera scanning after two frames shipped green through every automated test and through the self-test — this page is the check that would have caught it.

Open it on a device with a real camera and press start. In CI/headless, Chromium's --use-fake-device-for-media-stream also produces I420 and exercises the same path.
